Infisuite Finance Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Infisuite Finance is an accounting app for a company workspace. This policy explains what the app stores and why. It applies to the mobile app and the website.

Information we store

  • Account details you enter to sign in: email address, password, and optional company code or GSTIN. The password is stored as a hash, not as plain text.
  • Work you save in the app: vouchers, invoices, ledgers, payroll, bank, tax, and other company records.
  • A session token on the device or browser so you stay signed in until you log out.

Camera

If you use Scan UPI, the phone camera is used only to read a QR code. The app does not keep a photo library from that scan.

How it is used

This information is used to sign you in, show your company’s books, and save the records you create. It is not sold. It is not used for advertising.

Who can see it

People in your company workspace can see records according to their role. The app operator can access the server that stores this data in order to run the service. The server address used by the app is https://finstaq.digixlabs.io.

How long it is kept

Company records stay until a company owner or the operator deletes them. Logging out removes the session token from that phone or browser. It does not delete the company books.

Contact

To ask about this data or request a correction, contact the Infisuite administrator who created your login.
